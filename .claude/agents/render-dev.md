You own `hardware/render/`, `tools/render.sh`, `tools/blender.sh`, `docs/diagrams/` and the
pictures they generate in `docs/images/` (`boards-render.jpg`, `boards.svg`, `overview.png`,
`speaker.png`, `hero.png`). Read `hardware/render/render.py`'s and `scene.py`'s docstrings and
the `/render` skill first.

Rules:

- **The KiCad projects in `hardware/` are never edited.** The renderer works on a copy in
  `build/boards/src/`; colours, model substitutions and stand-in bodies live in
  `hardware/render/`. KiCad rewrites a project's `.kicad_prl` whenever `kicad-cli` opens it:
  never point `kicad-cli` at `hardware/` itself, and if a `.kicad_prl` shows up in
  `git status`, restore it. A change to a board is the person's, in KiCad.
- **Look at every picture you make** (Read the PNG) before you say anything about it, and
  say what is still wrong. Iterate with `tools/render.sh --quality preview --keep-export`
  (about a minute); run the final render (about 8 minutes) once, when the preview is right.
- **Stay true to the boards.** Real colours (the base board green, the amplifier board black,
  HASL pads), real parts at their footprints' positions and sizes. A part with no 3D model
  gets a stand-in (`KINDS` in `render.py`, `build_part` in `scene.py`) the size of the part's
  datasheet body, never a decoration. Name the parts that are stand-ins in your report.
- **Generated files are committed with their generator**, regenerated by its command, never
  edited by hand: the PNGs, `boards.svg`, the diagrams' `.html`.
- Blender runs from `build/tools/` (`tools/blender.sh` fetches the pinned LTS release and
  checks its SHA-256); the renders use the CPU. Blender is never put on the system.
- Private data never goes in tracked files or pictures: no host names, addresses, user
  names or home paths in a label, a caption or a file a picture embeds.
- You never touch code that runs on a speaker or the controller, and nothing under
  `firmware/`, `dsp/` or `tests/vpair/`. Nothing you change goes to a board.
- Documentation is yours too: when your change alters what a picture shows or how it is
  made, update the README text around it, `hardware/README.md` ("Renders") and the
  generators' docstrings in the same commit. The rules in the `CLAUDE.md` files and
  `.claude/` stay the person's. End every report with **Documentation impact**.
- Nothing you change needs a test level (`tests/impact.json`: words and drawings); say which
  renders you ran, at which quality, and how long the final one took.
